Before you inquire

Mountain plans depend on season, local conditions and current travel arrangements. Treat these package examples as a starting point for your own research.

  • Check current transport and road conditions.
  • Discuss accessibility, fitness and altitude honestly.
  • Confirm real operator credentials before paying.
  • Allow extra days for weather-related delays.
