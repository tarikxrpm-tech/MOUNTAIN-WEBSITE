THE SKARDU EXPERIENCE

Gateway to a world of contrasts

Skardu is the principal town of Baltistan and a starting point for journeys toward the Karakoram’s high peaks. Around the town, the landscape shifts from the broad Indus valley to water-filled basins, rocky slopes and the extraordinary high plateau of Deosai. The town itself offers a useful base for acclimatisation, supplies and cultural visits.

Set aside enough time to travel slowly. Distances can feel short on a map yet take longer on mountain roads, and weather can disrupt flights and road travel alike. Local advice is essential when planning day trips, especially to high and remote areas.

Places to explore

  • Satpara Lake: a striking lake near Skardu with changing light and mountain reflections.
  • Shangrila / Lower Kachura: a popular lakeside stop; confirm current access and services.
  • Deosai National Park: a high plateau with seasonal wildflowers and Sheosar Lake; access is seasonal.
  • Skardu Fort (Kharpocho): a historic viewpoint above the town.
  • Shigar Valley: a cultural and landscape excursion from Skardu, including historic architecture.

Activities & travel tips

Plan lakeside walks, a guided visit to local heritage sites, photography, and a carefully timed Deosai excursion in its open season. Bring windproof layers even in summer, sun protection, comfortable shoes and motion-sickness remedies if needed. Respect wildlife in protected areas and use designated waste facilities.

Safety & access

High-altitude roads can be rough, isolated and weather-sensitive. Travel with experienced local drivers, check park opening status and road reports, and never assume a route is open based on an old itinerary. Take altitude seriously, keep water and warm clothing available, and carry a flexible schedule for disruptions.

Good to know

Skardu offers significant altitude changes between town and nearby plateaus; plan gradual activity.

  • Base: Skardu town
  • Allow: 4–7 days for local sights
  • Pack: sun protection and windproof layers
  • Deosai access: seasonal, verify locally
